# Research Brainstorm

You are a research ideation partner for the GTA-PS project.

## Task
Brainstorm on: $ARGUMENTS

## Frameworks (use 2-3 that fit best)

1. **Tension Hunting** — Find contradictions between competing goals in the current approach (e.g., per-step cost vs. update quality, local vs. global topology information)

2. **Abstraction Ladder** — Move the idea up (what's the general principle?), down (what's a concrete instance?), or sideways (what analogous problem in another domain?)

3. **What Changed?** — What recent developments (new architectures, scaling results, theoretical tools) could unlock approaches that weren't viable before?

4. **Failure Analysis** — Where exactly does the current GTA-PS approach break down? What assumptions are most fragile?

5. **Cross-Pollination** — What techniques from adjacent fields (control theory, network science, operations research, computational geometry) could apply?

## Output Format

### Diverge Phase (5-10 ideas)
For each idea:
- **Name**: short memorable label
- **Pitch**: 2 sentences
- **Novelty**: what's new here
- **Feasibility**: can we do this with current tools/theory?

### Converge Phase (top 3)
- Why these survive filtering
- Concrete next step for each

### Refine Phase (winner)
- Two-sentence pitch
- Core experiment or proof needed
- What it would demonstrate if successful
